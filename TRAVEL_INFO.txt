BHUBANESWAR TRANSPORT INFORMATION

Airport: Biju Patnaik International Airport (BBI)
Railway: Bhubaneswar Railway Station (BBS)
Bus: Babasaheb Bhimrao Ambedkar Bus Terminal (BSABT), commonly called Baramunda ISBT.

The page intentionally does not publish travel distances or journey times because these vary by route and traffic. Each card opens Google Maps for current directions.
